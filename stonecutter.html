The Stonecutter

"When nothing seems to help, I go and look at a stonecutter hammering away at his rock perhaps a hundred times without as much as a crack showing in it. Yet at the hundred and first blow it will split in two — and I know it was not that blow that did it, but all that had gone before." Jacob Riis

Answers the objection

The question in the room

"We've been in this three years. The metric is flat, the field looks unchanged, and I can't point to what the last two raises bought. Why fund another round into something that hasn't moved?"

Because the flat stretch is not the absence of progress — it is the progress. Every case on the next page sat exactly where you are now, indistinguishable from failure, right up until the blow that split the rock. You are not being asked to buy the crack. You are being asked to fund the ninety blows that make the hundred-and-first land.

The through-line

Each of these looked like capital and effort vanishing into a void. The outcome accrued to whoever understood they were funding the conditions for an inflection — not buying a near-term result.

The ask is not a 2028 outcome. It is a position in the infrastructure that makes the outcome possible. The entire return premium exists because it requires conviction through the illegible middle — the long interval in which the curve is flat, the doubters are loudest, and the position is cheapest.

The cases

On the data

Four cases — Deep learning, Marriage equality, Amazon, and Tesla — carry real, sourced figures, shown in each marker and the "real numbers" callout. Where a case is marked ◆ real data, the cited figures are actual.

The plotted curves render the shape of the dynamic on a common 0–100 index so cases stay comparable: each case's own trajectory is normalized — starting state to 0, the achieved outcome to 100 — over the span of its sourced window. The dotted guide is the idealized stonecutter pattern, not a data series. The remaining cases are illustrative, drawn to the same pattern and never given real numbers.

Each case carries its own survivorship caveat by design. The pattern is only legible in hindsight; for every rock that split, others were struck exactly as hard and never cracked. The discipline is not "hold everything forever" — it is knowing which flat stretch is a stonecutter and which is just a rock.
